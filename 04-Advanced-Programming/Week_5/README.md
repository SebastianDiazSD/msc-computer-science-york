# Week 5 – Advanced Programming: Professionalism, Ethics & Impact

## Overview
Week 5 takes a step back from programming to examine the **broader responsibilities** of computing professionals. Through three real-world case studies, the week explores the moral, ethical, legal, and societal dimensions of software systems and their impact across social, environmental, economic, and educational contexts.

## Case Studies Covered
- **Volkswagen Emissions Scandal** – deliberate misuse of software to falsify emissions data
- **Driverless Cars** – autonomous systems and unresolved legal/ethical liability
- **Bias in Algorithms** – filter bubbles, discriminatory ML models, and the ethics of algorithmic gatekeeping

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this week you should be able to:
1. Demonstrate an awareness of the moral, ethical and legal issues that can arise from innovations in computer science.
2. Critically evaluate the impact of computer science innovations on environmental, social, economic and educational contexts.
3. Identify and present with supporting evidence opposing perspectives on challenging issues currently faced by the field of computer science.
